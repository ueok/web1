REGULATION & COMPLIANCE

Indonesia Vape Policy & What It Means for You

We build compliance into every unit. Here's the landscape as of October 2026.

📜 Legal Status

E-cigarettes are legal but strictly regulated in Indonesia. Under BPOM Regulation No. 18/2025 (issued 26 Jul 2025, mandatory from 26 Jul 2026), vapes are defined as "Other Processed Tobacco Products" (HPTL) — regulated like conventional tobacco, covering both natural and synthetic nicotine.

🏛️ Who Regulates

BPOM (Food & Drug Authority), Ministry of Trade, Customs (Bea Cukai), Ministry of Industry and Ministry of Health. Selling or importing requires recommendation letters, SNI certification, an API importer license and trade-import approval.

🧪 Testing & SNI

Every variant (flavor/strength/formula) needs nicotine-content testing by a BPOM-accredited lab. Additives are banned unless proven safe, with two-stage testing by two independent labs. SNI standards: e-liquids SNI 9070:2022, heated tobacco SNI 8946:2021.

📦 Packaging & Labeling

Health warnings must cover 50% of front & back (black background, yellow "Peringatan" text), with 5 warning-image sets per variant. Must show nicotine content, ingredients, capacity (ml), "Mengandung nikotin" and the 21+ / no-pregnant-women statement. Size limits: closed pods ≤2 ml & ≤2/pack; open-system bottles 10 or 20 ml.

📢 Advertising Rules

Social-media advertising is banned; e-commerce must enforce 21+ age verification; offline sales are prohibited within 200 m of schools; promotion and sponsorship are heavily restricted.

💰 Excise & Tax

Under Finance Ministry Reg PMK 96/2024 (eff. 1 Jan 2025): heated tobacco 6,240 IDR/g, open-system e-liquid 1,368 IDR/ml, pod refills 41,983 IDR/pod — plus 5% import duty and 11% VAT on the domestic market. Export-bound production in the FTZ is structured to avoid Indonesian domestic excise.

⚠️ Informational summary only — vape regulations change and vary by destination market (US PMTA, EU TPD, etc.). Confirm specifics with BPOM, a licensed Indonesian legal counsel, and your target-market authority before production or import. LOGISTICS & SHIPPING

From Our Line to Your Market

Batam's free-trade status turns shipping from a cost center into a competitive edge.

⚓ Batu Ampar Port

Batam's main public international cargo port (~300k TEU/yr, expanding to 10 m draft). The primary export gateway for the island's manufacturing base, facing straight onto Singapore's shipping network.

🛢️ Kabil Port

Private industrial port for heavy industry, energy logistics and liquid cargo (LNG, oils), with 10–15 m draft for deep-draft vessels and integrated CIQP clearance.

✈️ Hang Nadim Airport

Indonesia's longest runway (4,025 m) — air-freight option for samples, small-batch and time-critical shipments to global hubs.

🔁 FTZ Re-Export

Goods can be stored, processed and assembled in-zone, then exported without Indonesian import duties — slashing landed cost and cash-flow pressure versus in-territory production.
