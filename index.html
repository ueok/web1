Why Batam

Indonesia's only island with both a Free Trade Zone and a Special Economic Zone — sitting on the world's busiest shipping lane, 20 km from Singapore.

Batam is the largest city in the Riau Islands, directly across the strait from Singapore and inside the Malacca–Singapore chokepoint that carries a third of global trade. It is Indonesia's manufacturing launchpad: duty-free inputs, one-window licensing, deep-water ports and an international airport — all outside the Pacific Ring of Fire, so there is no earthquake, tsunami or volcanic risk to your production line.

03

Compliance, handled

Indonesia regulates e-cigarettes as tobacco. We build your products to pass — from nicotine testing to packaging, excise and export paperwork.

  1. 2025-07-26BPOM 18/2025 issued

    E-cigarettes defined as HPTL, regulated like cigarettes. Natural & synthetic nicotine both covered.

  2. 2026-07-26Fully mandatory

    One-year transition ends; the 2013 framework is repealed. Non-compliant products are delisted.

Testing & ingredients

Every variant (flavour / strength / formula) needs nicotine testing by a BPOM-approved lab. Additives are banned unless proven safe, with pre-market and in-market cross-checks by two separate labs.

Packaging & labels

50% front-and-back health warnings (black on yellow), 5 rotating warning sets, “contains nicotine” and “not for under-21 or pregnant” statements, plus production code, ingredients, nicotine strength and volume. No promotional wording.

Standards (SNI)

E-liquids to SNI 9070:2022, heated tobacco to SNI 8946:2021. Volume limits per BPOM 28/2024: closed pods ≤2 ml, open bottles 10/20 ml, solid sticks in 20-stick packs.

Market entry

Recommendation letters from BPOM, Ministry of Health and Ministry of Industry; API importer licence and Ministry of Trade import approval; pre-market notification (~7 working days completeness review).

Excise (PMK 96/2024)

Solid e-cigarettes IDR 6,240/g · open-system liquid IDR 1,368/ml · closed-system liquid IDR 41,983/pod, plus import duty and VAT on top. We model landed cost into your quote.

Advertising limits

No social-media advertising; e-commerce requires 21+ age verification; sales banned within 200 m of schools. We keep your go-to-market plan inside the rules.

Destination markets too

Beyond Indonesia, we prepare documentation and formulations for the markets you actually sell in — including EU TPD, US PMTA, UK MHRA and Middle-East registration regimes.

06

FAQ

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Typically 3,000–5,000 units per SKU for disposables and pods, and 500–1,000 units for custom e-liquid runs. Send your target volume for an exact figure.

How long does production and delivery take?

Sampling takes 7–14 days. After artwork and formula approval, mass production runs 25–35 days, plus sea or air freight from Batam.

Can you help with BPOM, SNI and export compliance?

Yes. We build products to meet BPOM 18/2025 and SNI standards (SNI 9070:2022 for e-liquids, SNI 8946:2021 for heated tobacco) and support destination requirements such as EU TPD and US PMTA.

Why manufacture in Batam instead of elsewhere?

Batam is Indonesia's only island with both a Free Trade Zone and a Special Economic Zone. Inputs are duty- and VAT-free, finished goods can be re-exported without Indonesian import duty, and the island sits 20 km from Singapore on the main Europe–Asia lane.

What product types can you produce?

Disposables, closed pod systems, open/refillable systems, e-liquid filling, solid sticks and heated-tobacco formats — with in-house R&D, flavour lab, tooling and QC.
